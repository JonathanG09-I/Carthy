What is Carthy?

A complete student wellness platform

Carthy connects students with trained listeners through active listening sessions in a safe, confidential, and accessible environment.

Created for educational communities, it helps students who need someone to talk to—without stigma, long waits, or complicated processes.

Beyond scheduling, Carthy includes mood tracking, secure chat, wellness resources, and tools that support emotional well-being every day.

Listen first. Support always.
